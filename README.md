# FoodBev Hub

We are going to create a portal that will be called food n bev. It will allow users as well as the admin to put info on the industrial constructions projects in the food and beverage industry. These could be newbuilds or extensions, refurbishments or modifications of already existing facilities. Facilities include food processing facility, brewery or distillery. The food processing facility could be meat, fish, snacks, coldroom or other. 




The portal will be centered around the projects. The mandatory project details will include:




Name of the facility - for example, "Brewdog Brewery”

Location of the facility - full address ideally

Brief description - production line expansion 

Status - planning, underway, completed or unknown




The following details are optional:

End-user in charge - contact details of the end user in charge, name email phone number 

Architect - company, contact person at the firm, email and phone number 

General Constructor - company, contact person at the firm, email and phone number 

M&E - company, contact person at the firm, email and phone number 

Real estate planner - company, contact person at the firm, email and phone number 

Consultant - company, contact person at the firm, email and phone number 

Flooring Company - company, contact person at the firm, email and phone number 

Groundworkers/Civil Workers - company, contact person at the firm, email and phone number

Drainage - company, contact person at the firm, email and phone number 

Other (allow multiple other subcontractors) - description, company, contact person at the firm, email and phone number 




With each category, there should also be an optional place for the description of a product/service specification.




The portal should have an extensive search functions (keyword, area, company, architect, type of product etc) 




Each project should have a picture automatically pulled from google ideally if the address is included or a placeholder with a picture that represents the type of facility. 




Users will also be able to score each project in two separate ratings - how hot is the project and how accurate the information are.The third score will be awarded automatically by the algorithm based on how complete the information is - if the project has quite complete info and description it will get more percentage. The three scores are simple 0 to 100 percent, with 100 percent being the best score and 0 percent the worst. 




Users who are not registered will only be able to see the name of the facility, the location, brief description, status and whether or not there are any optional fields for example architects - yes, general constructors - no, but without any specific details. Once registered and logged in, users will be able to:




View all the information 

Add and remove projects (but only remove projects which they have created)

Add information - they can add and remove information to the projects someone else created, but they can only add info in places where info has not been added yet and remove the information only they have added. For example if someone else has already put info about architects they can’t remove it or modify it, but they can add “Other”, in the description put architects and add new details

In the registration alias, email and are you involved/hoping to get involved: yes/no will be mandatory, full name and mobile are optional 




The colour palette should include a mix of HEX colours, #eab676, #abdbe3, black and white
